# Learning library: methods, not market proof

Use these sources to choose a discovery method, sharpen a question, or design an experiment. Do not use them to prove demand, willingness to pay, market size, product legality, competitor absence, or country-specific behavior.

## Evidence separation

| Layer | Proper use | Must not prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder evidence | Learn from interviews, pilots, sales, and observations | General trends beyond its sample |
| Current market research | Establish competitor, regulatory, pricing, and adoption facts | A universal product method |
| Learning library | Select methods and discovery questions | Demand, TAM, or legality |
| Model inference | State an interpretation of the above | Fact |

When applying a source, cite it under `Methods used` and say what it does **not** establish. Use at most two framework sources per substantive report. Prefer local customer evidence if it conflicts with generic advice.

| [Y Combinator Startup Library](https://www.ycombinator.com/library) | Early-stage fundamentals, ideas, customer conversations | General framework, not a country prescription. |

## Routing guide

| Founder question | Relevant methods | Evidence still needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is this a real customer problem? | Techstars, YC, Antler | Interviews, workaround, frequency, stakes |
| What belongs in the MVP? | Antler, Techstars, YC | Task completion, pilot behavior, commitment |
| How do we sell B2B software? | Alchemist, Techstars | Buyer map, procurement, pricing, security/regulation |
| Do we have founder-market fit? | EF, Antler | Access, lived insight, trusted distribution, learning speed |
| Can this work in an African market? | MEST, VC4A, Google for Startups Africa | Country payment, trust, regulation, connectivity, distribution data |
| Is an impact thesis commercially viable? | Village Capital, 500 Global | User/beneficiary/buyer separation, unit economics, outcomes |
| Are we ready to raise? | Techstars, Antler, YC, 500 Global | Traction, retention, sales evidence, market access, capital plan |

## Source-card format

When a host supports a framework library, store:

```text
provider
title
canonical_url
topic_tags
market_tags
content_type
published_or_checked_at
rights_or_access_note
short_original_summary
approved_for_retrieval
```

Retrieve at most two relevant source cards for a report. Keep framework references visibly separate from factual sources.
