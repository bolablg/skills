# Job evaluation

## Decision waterfall

Stop at the first hard disqualifier:

1. Closed, duplicate, inaccessible, or likely fraudulent posting.
2. Country is absent from a configured market, or the location/work mode conflicts with that market.
3. A remote employer does not accept the candidate's configured residence country, or relocation conditions conflict.
4. Country-specific authorization/sponsorship is incompatible or unconfirmed for an application.
5. Disclosed compensation ceiling below the configured market/currency floor.
6. Role family or seniority clearly outside target scope.
7. Posting older than the configured limit.
8. Match below the configured threshold.

Unknown salary is not a rejection unless salary transparency is required. Unknown legal eligibility may remain during research, but it blocks application when the role or form requires the answer.

## Evidence-grounded match score

| Dimension | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Core responsibilities | 25 |
| Required skills and tools | 25 |
| Experience and seniority | 20 |
| Domain or industry | 10 |
| Location and work model | 10 |
| Preferred qualifications | 10 |

For every positive score, cite the candidate resource and exact evidence. Do not give credit merely because a résumé uses a similar keyword. Apply explicit deductions for missing hard requirements, but represent uncertainty separately from mismatch.

## Decisions

- `qualified`: evidence meets the threshold and no blocker remains.
- `needs-review`: one consequential fact or ambiguous requirement needs the user.
- `skip`: record the first decisive reason.
- `closed` or `duplicate`: do not evaluate further.
- `possible-scam`: quarantine from application and explain the evidence.

Ranking is advisory. It does not permit fabricating qualifications or submitting without approval.

## Salary answers

Use only the strategy in the profile. Never invent salary history. If the profile says `ask`, stop before entering an answer. Preserve currency, period, and whether the employer requests base, total compensation, hourly rate, or a range.
