# Application and outreach writing

## Source discipline

Build an evidence map before writing. Each claim must trace to the résumé or another resource approved for the intended `use_for`. Mark conflicts and ask the candidate rather than silently preferring résumé, LinkedIn, or portfolio wording. Omit unsupported claims. Never invent metrics, technologies, dates, titles, education, certifications, legal eligibility, referrals, motivations, or personal stories.

Reading permission is not sharing permission. Mention, link, or attach a resource only when `share_with_employers` is true and the exact destination is approved.

## Résumé tailoring

Tailor by selecting, ordering, and phrasing verified evidence for the target role. Preserve the underlying facts and keep the candidate's intended seniority. Optimize for clarity and relevant terminology, not keyword stuffing. Keep an immutable copy of the original and a clearly named generated variant.

Read `resume-building-framework.md` for evidence reuse, baseline selection, priority mapping, achievement questions, factual distinctions, and proportionate outputs. Check confirmed achievements and previous answers before asking again. Start with the candidate's preferred baseline, improve the headline and summary first, and revise experience bullets only where the target or evidence warrants it. Explain the system or deliverable, its consumers, and its supported value. Keep personal contributions separate from team results and intended benefits separate from observed outcomes.

Read `resume-style-and-seniority-calibration.md` before drafting or formatting any application résumé. Use its classic single-column layout as the default and treat its overqualification gate as mandatory. Separate the factual chronology from positioning: preserve official titles, employers, and dates, but calibrate the summary, skills order, bullet selection, leadership emphasis, length, and quantified scale to the actual job level.

Generate a fresh application-specific résumé only after the job is verified, scored, and selected for application preparation. Do not create speculative application variants for stale, duplicate, blocked, or below-threshold roles. A requested general résumé review or draft can proceed without a selected job; label alignment and seniority calibration provisional. Default to two pages within the candidate's length preference and relevant evidence, with deliberate page balance and no padded content or stranded section headings.

Block the résumé when the summary claims a higher target level than the job, when manager/director language dominates an individual-contributor application, or when removing seniority risk would require hiding or falsifying chronology. Record the risk and use a manual handoff instead.

## Cover letters and short answers

Use a direct, specific voice. Most cover letters should be 150–250 words:

1. Name the role and strongest evidence-backed fit.
2. Connect two or three verified accomplishments to the role's priorities.
3. Mention one inspected company/team/problem detail when useful.
4. Close with a simple next step.

Answer screening questions directly and within the character limit. Legal, authorization, salary-history, demographic, relocation, travel, and background questions must come from explicit profile values or be escalated.

## Quality checks

- Correct company, role, recipient, and destination.
- No content copied from a different application.
- Every accomplishment and tool is supported.
- Personal versus team contribution, prototype versus production, and observed versus intended results remain explicit.
- Metrics retain supported values, units, periods, and qualifiers; derived calculations stay in private evidence notes.
- Relevance, clarity, impact, and credibility reviewed; internal fit scores never presented as ATS results or hiring predictions.
- No hidden commitments or invented enthusiasm.
- No unnecessary sensitive information.
- Exact approved résumé and attachment names.
- Classic single-column style validation completed.
- Seniority and overqualification gate completed against the exact job description.
- No orphaned section heading, excessive second-page whitespace, or page-one crowding.
- Editable DOCX and final PDF saved; PDF text extraction and visual inspection of every rendered page recorded.
- Material changes, evidence sources, missing facts, and unverified checks recorded outside the résumé.
- Human-readable, concise language without generic AI filler.

For recruiter notes, lead with relevance and one differentiator. Do not attach a résumé or share contact details until the user approves the exact recipient and channel.
